What's in this ocean space?

Draw a box where a wind farm might go. You'll see, instantly:

  • how NOAA's 2023 siting model rated the area, and why
  • how much of the most important fishing ground it covers
  • which ports are closest and what they land
  • the coastal county economies tied to those ports

Model data covers the 2022 Coos Bay and Brookings Call Areas. Drag the box to move it, or drag a corner to resize.
